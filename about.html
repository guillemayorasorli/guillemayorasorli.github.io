About

I am Guillem Ayora Sorli, based in Barcelona. I work in strategy and investment analysis for a family-owned business group, run a small investment project and am preparing an MSc in Economics at Barcelona School of Economics (UPF-BSE).

This site collects field notes: things I notice in daily life, from a terrace in Barcelona to a podcast episode, followed by an attempt to test them against data and research. The aim is to explain how incentives, prices and choices show up in ordinary situations.

Background

Business (BBA with Honors, EAE Business School Barcelona), corporate banking experience and investment analysis. I also speak English, Spanish, Catalan and Japanese (ギレーム is how my name is written in Japanese), and I photograph: that work lives at guillemayora.com.
