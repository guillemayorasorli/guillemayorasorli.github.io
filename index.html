by Guillem Ayora Sorli Linkedin Aeris

Economist in training and strategy professional from Barcelona. I work with family businesses, invest with a sustainability lens and write about the economics hiding in everyday life. I am currently studying an MSc in Economics at the Barcelona School of Economics. Off the clock: sushi, Catan, and as many books as I can get my hands on.
